    At scale, and under failure

    One document is easy. The benchmark seeds 1,000 documents, then rewrites 10% of all paragraphs at 50 edits per second with two indexer workers.

    Edit visible in search, median158 ms
    Edit visible in search, 95th percentile376 ms
    Chunks re-embedded806 of 4,817
    Embedding calls avoided83%

    The fault-injection suite runs the real services and breaks them on purpose. After every scenario a verifier recomputes the whole index from the source documents and must find nothing lost, nothing applied twice and no orphaned chunks.

    • Indexer workers killed in the middle of writes
    • Every event delivered twice
    • Events shuffled across partitions, then replayed backwards
    • 30% of embedding calls failing
    • The outbox relay killed three times mid-run

    Built by Nireeksha Huns. Embeddings on this page come from a local deterministic model, so similarity scores are rough; the timings are real.
